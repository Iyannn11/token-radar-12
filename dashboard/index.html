How the implied value is worked out

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier

  • Category multiple. For each category, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in that category whose token already trades (at least three of them). A category with too few live comps falls back to the global median across all live-token protocols. The multiple is recomputed from this snapshot on every run — no protocol name is ever special-cased, and no multiple is hardcoded.
  • Growth multiplier. 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%) ÷ 100 × 0.25. Growth is a whisper here: a shrinking protocol is barely punished, because the thesis is about waiting, not momentum.
  • Maturity multiplier. 0.3 + min(age in days, 730) ÷ 730 × 0.7. Age is the story: a protocol that has run for two years with no token is far closer to a launch than one listed last month, so the maturity factor spans 0.3 to 1.0 over two years.
  • What it is not. The implied value is a model of what a launch could be worth, not a price and not a promise. It says nothing about whether a token will launch at all.
